    EPISODE 01

    Joystick to Neural Net

    This arm has no brain. It won't move until you tell it to. Every motion starts as a keypress, travels down a wire as a command, and runs on the motors a moment later.

    That's teleoperation: a human in the loop, watching, commanding, and correcting in real time. It's how robots get driven, and it's how they get taught.

    Pick up the crystal and set it inside the ring. Then turn up the latency and try again.
